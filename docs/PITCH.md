# Til Loan Day — pitch pack

## 1. BuilderBase one-liners

1. **Recommended:** Til Loan Day makes your student loan last till the next one: see what you can safely spend, preview plans before you say yes, and sort housemate money fairly.
2. Til Loan Day helps students see how shifts and bills change their daily budget, decide before committing, and split rooms, rent and bills with housemates.
3. Til Loan Day helps students turn group-chat invites and house bills into budget decisions they can check before saying yes.

## 2. Elevator pitch — 30 seconds

> The group chat wants an answer about the winter ball, but your balance doesn’t tell you whether you’ll make it to loan day. Til Loan Day shows students what they can spend each day and how saying yes, or picking up a shift, changes that number. When the house chat turns to who gets which room and who owes for the shop, Dibs helps you agree a fair rent split and settle shared bills.

## 6. Judge Q&A

| Question                                               | Short answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Why not just use Monzo’s “Left to Spend”?**       | We’re not replacing a bank’s budgeting tools. This demo answers a student’s specific question: what happens between now and my next loan if I say yes to this invitation or put money down on a house? You can try it without a bank login.             |
| **2. Is Mon 11 Jan really when my loan lands?**        | Not necessarily. Mon 11 Jan 2027 is NTU’s standard undergraduate Term Two start and the editable demo estimate. Check your own Student Finance England payment schedule and enter your date.                                                            |
| **3. Can Dibs be gamed?**                              | Don’t assume it’s strategy-proof. A housemate can misstate what a room is worth to them. Dibs can only check the split against the preferences and limits people actually enter.                                                                        |
| **4. Where does the AI send my message?**              | If you use AI extraction, the pasted text goes through Lovable’s AI gateway. That is not the same as sending a reply to your group chat; you control that. The fallback parses on-device. Don’t paste sensitive details you don’t need for the preview. |
| **5. How do you know the maths is right?**             | `/selftest` runs the engines’ acceptance checks live: **28/28 PASS** in the demo. The maths is also checked against an exact-fraction reference on hundreds of random cases. That is test evidence, not proof for every possible input.                 |
| **6. What if I have no arranged overdraft?**           | Use a zero limit. There’s no borrowing buffer in the forecast: the question is when your own money runs out, not when you start relying on an overdraft you don’t have.                                                                                 |
| **7. What happens if no fair split fits our budgets?** | Dibs says it can’t fit and surfaces the gap, for example, “You’re 50p a week short.” It doesn’t quietly assign someone a rent over their limit.                                                                                                         |
| **8. Is the holding-deposit cap per person?**          | No. For a covered let in England it’s one week’s rent per tenancy. On a joint tenancy that’s a single cap for the household, not one cap per housemate. The £95 preview is your share in the demo, not the legal cap.                                   |
| **9. What if the AI gets the date or price wrong?**    | The extracted fields are there to check and correct before previewing. If AI is slow or unavailable, the on-device parser fills the fields; the demo chips can run without a network.                                                                   |
| **10. Does “safe to spend” mean I can’t run short?**   | No. It’s a forecast from your inputs, not a guarantee. If the payment date, bills or spending change, update the inputs. An arranged overdraft is borrowing, not extra income.                                                                          |
| **11. Isn’t asking parents for money a bit much?**     | It’s an editable draft about a specific bill, with the amount and due date included. You decide whether to ask, how to phrase it and who receives it.                                                                                                   |
| **12. Is paying rent upfront allowed or advisable?**   | We don’t give legal or financial advice; the app shows the effect on your money. The holding-deposit cap is a separate rule: see [the fact-check](FACT-CHECK.md#2-holding-deposits-in-england).                                                         |
| **13. Can others see my XP?**                          | Not unless you share it. XP is stored only on your device, with no accounts or leaderboard. To compare with a friend, you export a friend-card file with the fields you choose and send it yourself.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **14. What if the shifts don’t happen?**               | Income is your own estimate, not a prediction or bank transaction. Change or remove it if your plans change.                                                                                                                                            |
| **15. Does it find my subscriptions for me?**          | No. You enter them yourself. Monthly subscriptions count as bills before loan day; there’s no bank connection by design.                                                                                                                                |
| **16. Is the AI’s habit price right?**                 | It’s an estimate. You see and edit the rows before using the daily figure, and nothing changes your runway until you choose to use it.                                                                                                                  |
| **17. Where is my friend data?**                       | Only in the file you chose to send, and on your device. There are no accounts and no server copy; you pick which fields go in the friend card.                                                                                                          |

## 7. Don’t say

- **“The maths is proven” or “Dibs can’t be gamed.”** Say what the tests cover and that the split depends on reported preferences and limits.
- **“Everyone’s signing this week.”** Show why a student considering a house could use the preview this week; don’t claim universal behavior.
- **“No other tool does this.”** Describe what Dibs checks. The fair-rent maths is inspired by Spliddit (Carnegie Mellon University) and _Fair Rent Division on a Budget_ (Procaccia, Velez & Yu).
- **“SFE pays everyone on Mon 11 Jan.”** Say it’s the editable NTU Term Two demo default; students should check their own schedules.
- **“A week’s rent per person” or “the £95 is the legal cap.”** The cap is per tenancy for covered lets in England; £95 is the demo share.
- **“Overdrafts are free money” or “everyone has one.”** Amber is borrowing within the agreed limit. Without an arrangement there’s no buffer to use up.
- **“Everything stays on-device” or “the AI sends the reply for you.”** AI extraction uses Lovable’s gateway; the fallback is on-device, and the user decides what to send.
- **“We save a measured number of minutes,” “it’s business software,” or “this is an average meal-deal price.”** Describe the steps saved, be clear this is student admin, and leave meal-deal prices out of the pitch.
- **“It detects your subscriptions” or “it’s connected to your bank.”** Subscriptions and expected income are entered by the student; there’s no bank connection.
